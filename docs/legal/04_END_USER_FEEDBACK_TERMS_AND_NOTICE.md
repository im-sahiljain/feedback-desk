# End-User Feedback Terms & Transparency Notice

**Applicability:** Displayed to consumers accessing single-use feedback forms (`/f/invite/:token`) and counter QR standee portals (`/f/scan/:productId`).

---

## 1. Transparency & Purpose of Feedback

This feedback portal is powered by **FeedbackDesk Technologies** on behalf of the business or product named on this page ("**the Business**").

By submitting your rating and comments:
1. **Direct Communication:** Your feedback is delivered directly to the management team of the Business to improve service quality, resolve complaints, and enhance customer experience.
2. **Authenticity Guarantee:** You certify that your submission represents your genuine, first-hand experience with the Business as a bona fide customer or patron.
3. **No Incentivized Manipulation:** Submissions must not be made in exchange for unauthorized monetary payments, conditional discounts, or under coercion.

---

## 2. Immutability & Single-Use Invariant

* **Write-Once Record:** To protect the integrity and authenticity of reviews, once you click **Submit**, your response is permanently recorded and your unique single-use link is deactivated. Submissions cannot be edited or resubmitted.
* **Anti-Spam Shield:** To prevent review-bombing or automated duplicate submissions, this service verifies that identical reviews are not submitted repeatedly.

---

## 3. Privacy & How Your Information Is Handled

1. **Optional Contact Details:** Providing your email address is entirely optional. If provided, the Business may use it to contact you regarding your feedback or resolve your concern.
2. **Automated Privacy Scrubbing:** Our system automatically filters and redacts sensitive personal data (such as bank details, payment cards, and government ID numbers) before processing. Please do not include confidential passwords or financial account numbers in your comments.
3. **No Invasive Tracking:** This platform does not install advertising cookies, profile your cross-site browsing behavior, or capture invasive device hardware fingerprints.

---

## 4. Consumer Rights & Inquiries

If you wish to request the deletion or correction of feedback you submitted, please contact the Business directly or submit an inquiry to `privacy@feedbackdesk.com` referencing your approximate submission date and the business name.
